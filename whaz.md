## Target Audience
- **Students**: Individuals looking for early-stage career direction, CV guidance, and entry-level profile improvement.

- **Freelancers & Creators**: Independent professionals looking for practical strategies to scale their services, manage client interactions, or build personal branding solutions.

- **Founders & Entrepreneurs**: Early-stage business starters who need help with idea validation, growth planning, and building visual or operational roadmaps (their "Venture Blueprint" service).

- **Young Professionals**: Job seekers looking for strategic profile optimization, decision-making support, and structured action plans to achieve goal clarity.


## Why Whaz
Whaz complements mission-focused leadership by providing an execution layer that combines AI insights with human expertise. The platform helps organizations improve operational maturity, align strategic priorities, and measure progress without compromising their mission.
